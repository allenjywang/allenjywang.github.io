Hi there! 

I am a first year PhD student of team [NEO](https://team.inria.fr/neo/fr/) at Inria, supervised by Prof. [Giovanni Neglia](https://www-sop.inria.fr/members/Giovanni.Neglia/) and Prof. [Chuan Xu](https://sites.google.com/view/chuanxu). My research is funded by the European Union Horizon MSCA DN programme [FINALITY](https://www.finality-dn.eu/).

Previously, I obtained my master degree and bachelor degree from ShanghaiTech University and South China University of Technology, both in Computer Science. My current research interest is robust federated learning.

In my spare time, I enjoy reading, working out, travelling, and sometimes writing. Please feel free to share interesting things with me!
